Implemented a non-data-driven accounting DAG method under accounting/graph.py.

Method:
- Source nodes include revenue, total_assets, and a cash-flow source.
- The DAG is expanded using BFS with configurable depth.
- Each directed edge is defined as child_value / parent_value.
- Missing child values, missing parent values, zero denominators, NaN, and inf are defaulted to 0.
- Extreme ratios are clipped to avoid unstable neural-network inputs.
- The DAG is linearized into a fixed-size vector for both TTM and quarterly accounting data.

Deliverables:
1. Depth sweep from 1 to 4 is saved in accounting/outputs/depth_sweep.csv.
2. Graph images and JSON adjacency lists are generated for 2020-04-01 and 2026-04-01.
3. Each ticker has an individual graph, and each date also has a cross-asset graph.
4. Validation error is stratified by ticker and over time.
5. Validation includes balance sheet deviation, cash flow deviation, income statement deviation, cumulative error, and MAE.

Main break cases:
- CCIV has the highest average MAE, mainly driven by balance sheet deviation. This is likely related to SPAC / ticker-transition behavior and unusual balance sheet structure.
- LCID also has elevated error after the CCIV transition.
- SIVB has the highest income-statement deviation, likely because bank accounting differs from standard operating-company accounting.
- BBBY has the highest cash-flow deviation, likely due to distressed retail cash-flow instability.
- Overall, the method is more stable for standard operating companies and less stable for SPACs, financial firms, distressed firms, or tickers with sparse / unusual accounting fields.
