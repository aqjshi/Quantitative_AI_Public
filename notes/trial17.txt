Typical Case (1-2 changes): O(klogn) where k is the number of changes. 
For a 27-year span (n≈10,000 days), logn is only 13-14 calls. If a stock changes 3 times, you're looking at ~40 calls.

Worst Case (Total Chaos): If a stock changed every single day, it would devolve to O(n), but the SEC doesn't move that fast.
