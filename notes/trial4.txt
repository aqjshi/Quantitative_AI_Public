This architecture handles the transition of sovereign currencies into a unified monetary union (e.g., the 1999 Euro transition) by reconfiguring the Graph Neural Network (GNN) topology without losing historical learning.

3. Stability and Distribution Management

A major risk in merging nodes is "Distribution Collapse." Simply summing weight vectors can alter the variance of the neural layer, leading to catastrophic failure (dead neurons or saturation).



WEIGHT DISTRIBUTIONS:
    
    [ Parent Weights ]       [ Naive Summation ]      [ Scaled Fusion ]

Implementation Risks:

    Decoupling Failure: Abruptly removing nodes can cause local network instability.

    Correlation Bias: This method assumes that real-world currency correlations are reflected in similar neural weight directions.
